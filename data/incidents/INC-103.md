# INC-103: Suspended members earning points

**Date:** 2024-09-05
**Severity:** SEV-3
**Service:** Rewards / Membership

## Summary
Members suspended within the last 10 minutes continued to earn points.

## Root cause
Rewards caches the Membership lookup for 10 minutes, so status changes were not seen
until the cache expired.

## Resolution
Membership now publishes `member.status.changed` events, and Rewards evicts the cached
member on receipt.
