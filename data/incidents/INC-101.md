# INC-101: Points expired early for leap-year transactions

**Date:** 2024-03-02
**Severity:** SEV-2
**Service:** Rewards

## Summary
Members who earned points on 2023-03-01 saw them expire on 2024-02-29, one day earlier
than the policy promised customers ("one year after purchase").

## Root cause
`RewardExpirationService.calculateExpiration()` adds a fixed 365 days to the transaction
date. Across a leap day, 365 days lands one calendar day short of the anniversary.

## Resolution
Restored the affected points manually. Product confirmed the official policy is
365 days, so the code was left unchanged and the customer-facing copy was updated
to say "365 days after purchase".
